# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Gamasa Properties serves two primary audiences with equal priority:

- Renters and visitors looking for apartments, rooms, chalets, villas, and studios in Gamasa, often from a phone and often comparing several options quickly.
- Property owners and landlords who need to publish listings, manage availability, follow bookings, and keep their property information trustworthy.

The product is Arabic-first, RTL-first, and should feel dependable on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

## Product Purpose

Gamasa Properties helps people discover, compare, save, book, and manage rental properties in Gamasa. Success means users can move from search to property details to booking without confusion, while owners can add and manage listings without feeling they are fighting the interface.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, practical. The interface should feel like a competent local real estate desk translated into a clean digital product: clear, steady, responsive, and respectful of the user's time.

## Anti-references

- Do not make the product feel like a generic SaaS landing page.
- Do not use heavy purple or blue-purple gradients as the dominant identity.
- Do not rely on decorative glassmorphism, nested cards, or noisy shadows.
- Do not hide important workflows on mobile.
- Do not make Arabic text feel squeezed, clipped, or secondary.

## Design Principles

- Put the next action within reach. Search, save, book, add, and manage should be easy to find on touch screens.
- Earn trust through clarity. Prices, location, status, and ownership actions must be readable before they are beautiful.
- Keep density useful. Desktop can show more information, but mobile should reveal progressively without removing core functions.
- Respect RTL. Alignment, icon direction, truncation, and keyboard flow must all feel native in Arabic.
- State is part of the product. Loading, empty, error, disabled, and offline states should guide the user instead of leaving them stranded.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG AA as the baseline. Maintain readable contrast, 44px minimum touch targets, visible focus states, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and resilient layouts for long Arabic labels, mixed Arabic/English numbers, and small mobile screens.
