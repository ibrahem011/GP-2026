## 2024-04-17 - Added Accessibility to View Toggle and Close Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only stateful toggle buttons (like map/list or list/grid views) often miss crucial context for screen readers. Using `aria-pressed` explicitly communicates their active state, while `aria-hidden="true"` on the inner icons prevents redundant or confusing screen reader announcements. Additionally, explicitly defining `focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:outline-none` on close buttons ensures consistent keyboard navigation visibility across different browsers.
**Action:** Always verify that interactive elements relying solely on icons have an appropriate `aria-label`, inner icons are marked `aria-hidden="true"`, stateful toggles use `aria-pressed`, and `focus-visible` styles are explicitly defined for keyboard users.
